Key uses: qrcode encodes standards-compliant QR matrices; jsqr decodes images locally; jsPDF and svg2pdf.js produce PDFs; JSZip bundles batch outputs; JsBarcode generates barcodes; PapaParse reads quoted CSV; yaml and diff implement developer utilities; Zod validates versioned design data. React, Vinext, Next-compatible APIs, Tailwind, Radix primitives and Lucide support the application interface.

Test dependencies include Vitest, Playwright, Testing Library, jsdom and sharp (Apache-2.0). Review complete dependency licenses before redistributing a bundled build.
